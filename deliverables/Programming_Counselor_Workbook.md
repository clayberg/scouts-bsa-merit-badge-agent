## 2. Pre-Class Prerequisite Checklist ('Bring Proof to Class')

- [ ] **Req 1 (Explain):** Safety. Do the following: (a) View the Personal Safety Awareness "Digital Safety" video (with your parent or guardian's permission). (b) Discuss first aid and prevention for the types of injuries that could occur during programming activities, including repetitive stress injuries and eyestrain.
- [ ] **Req 6 (Explain):** Careers. Do ONE of the following: (a) Explore careers related to this merit badge. Research one career to learn about the training and education needed, costs, job prospects, salary, job duties, and career advancement. Your research methods may include, with your parent or guardian's permission, an internet or library search, an interview with a professional in the field, or a visit to a location where people in this career work. Discuss with your counselor both your findings and what about this profession might make it an interesting career. (b) Explore how you could use knowledge and skills from this merit badge to pursue a hobby or healthy lifestyle. Research any training needed, expenses, and organizations that promote or support it. Discuss with your counselor what short-term and long-term goals you might have if you pursued this.

## 3. Hands-On Skill Station Verification Rubric

- [ ] **Station Req 5:** Project. With your counselor's guidance, select three different programming languages and development environments. For each subrequirement below, do the following: Write or modify a program using the indicated programming language and development environment. The program must take input and produce output based on computations and decisions made on the input. Debug and demonstrate the program to your counselor. Explain how each program processes inputs, makes decisions based on those inputs, and provides outputs based on computations and decision making. (a) In the first language and environment, write or modify a program, debug and demonstrate, and explain as above. (b) In the second language and environment, write or modify a program, debug and demonstrate, and explain as above. - **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Analytical Engine Concept: Ada Lovelace authored the earliest machine algorithm designed for mechanical calculation.
  - Compiler Invention Impact: Grace Hopper developed early language translation systems converting English words into bytecode.

- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**

  - Step 4 - Counselor Presentation: Explain milestone impacts on modern application development during your patrol review session.
- **Common Myth:** Computer programming began only after silicon microprocessors were invented in California research labs.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Ada Lovelace documented algorithmic process execution on paper long before electronic computers existed.

## 3. Hands-On Skill Station Verification Rubric

- [ ] **Station Req 3:** Do the following: (a) Review the characteristics of life jackets most appropriate for kayaking and understand why one must always be worn while paddling. Then demonstrate how to select and fit a life jacket for kayaking. (b) Review the importance of safety equipment such as a signal device, extra paddle, sponge, bilge pump, flotation bags, and throw bag.
- [ ] **Station Req 5:** Discuss the following: (a) Correct methods for the use of a kayak paddle (b) Parts of a paddle (c) The care and maintenance of a paddle
- [ ] **Station Req 6:** Using a properly equipped kayak with an open cockpit, a sit-on-top, or an inflatable kayak, do the following: (a) Safely capsize and perform a wet exit. (b) Reenter the kayak with assistance from a buddy boat. (c) Demonstrate a kayak-over-kayak rescue. (d) Demonstrate the HELP position. (e) Capsize the kayak, swim it and the paddle to shore, and empty water from the kayak with assistance if needed.
- [ ] **Station Req 7:** As a solo paddler, use a properly equipped kayak to demonstrate the following: (a) Forward stroke (b) Backstroke (c) Forward sweep (d) Reverse sweep (e) Draw stroke (f) Stern draw
- [ ] **Station Req 8:** As a solo paddler, use a properly equipped kayak to demonstrate the following: (a) Paddle a straight line for 15 to 20 boat lengths using appropriate strokes while maintaining trim and balance of the kayak. (b) Spin or pivot from a stationary position 180 degrees (half circle) to the right and left within two boat lengths. (c) Move abeam to the right 10 feet and to the left 10 feet. (d) Stop the boat in one boat length. (e) While maintaining forward motion, turn the kayak 90 degrees to the right and left. (f) Move the kayak backward three to four boat lengths using appropriate and effective reverse strokes. (g) Paddle the kayak in a buoyed figure 8 course around markers three to four boat lengths apart.

### Req 2: Swimmer Classification Test Requirement
**Official Requirement 2:** Before doing requirements 3 through 8, successfully complete the Scouting America swimmer test. Note: See the Swimming merit badge pamphlet for details about the Scouting America swimmer test.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Kayaking Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 11, p. 37)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 2 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Perform the swim classification test in designated, lifeguard monitored areas before any open water boating activities begin.
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** Why does the swimmer test require completing the distance without breaks or wall support?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Entry Assessment: Jump feet first into water over head height and surface easily.
  - Stroke Distance: Complete one hundred yards continuously with seventy five yards strong stroke.
  - Conclude test by maintaining calm resting back float for sixty seconds.
  - Classification Requirement: Retest annually to maintain active swimmer status for all boating events.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Feet First Jump: Plunge feet first into water over head height to test recovery.
  - Step 2 - Distance Strokes: Swim seventy five yards using a strong, steady forward crawl or breaststroke.
  - Step 3 - Resting Stroke: Complete twenty five yards using an easy, relaxing elementary backstroke smoothly.
  - Step 4 - Water Treading: Rest motionless on your back for sixty seconds while breathing normally.
- **Common Myth:** Paddlers wearing life jackets do not need to pass aquatics swimming tests.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** The jump into deep water proves a swimmer can handle disorienting accidental immersion.

#### Req 5a: Kayak Paddle Usage Mechanics
**[Parent Directive]:** *Discuss the following*
**Official Requirement 5a:** Correct methods for the use of a kayak paddle (b) Parts of a paddle (c) The care and maintenance of a paddle.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Kayaking Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 33, p. 67)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 5a Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Avoid planting paddle blades into rocky bottoms to push off, which snaps blades and ferrules.
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** For kayaking Requirement 5a (Correct methods for the use of a kayak paddle (b) Parts of a paddle (c) The care and), what key BSA pamphlet standard and safety or verification step must you explain or demonstrate to your counselor?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Position hands shoulder width apart with a relaxed control hand grip and loose opposite hand. Rotate the torso smoothly from the hips to pull the power face through water without arm fatigue.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Blade Orientation: Position concave spoon faces toward the rear with top edges angled forward.

## 2. Pre-Class Prerequisite Checklist ('Bring Proof to Class')

- [ ] **Req 7 (Prepare):** Do TWO of the following: (a) Design a stamp, cancellation, or cachet. (b) Visit a post office, stamp club, or stamp show with an experienced collector. Explain what you saw and learned. (c) Write a review of an interesting article from a stamp newspaper, magazine, book, or website (with your parent or guardian's permission). (d) Research and report on a famous stamp-related personality or the history behind a particular stamp. (e) Describe the steps taken to produce a stamp. Include the methods of printing, types of paper, perforation styles, and how they are gummed. (f) Prepare a two- to three-page display involving stamps. Using ingenuity, as well as clippings, drawings, etc., tell a story about the stamps and how they relate to history, geography, or a favorite topic of yours.

- Commemorative Souvenir Stamp Sheets: The USPS also issued the Northeast Deciduous Forest souvenir stamp sheet (with 10 separate stamp designs) (p. 12).
- As of 2021, more than 220 postal administrations around the world continue to issue stamps (p. 12).

### Req 4: Stamp Catalogs and Condition Grading
**Official Requirement 4 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Demonstrate the use of ONE standard catalog for several different stamp issues. Explain why catalog value can vary from the corresponding purchase price. (b) Explain the meaning of the term condition as used to describe a stamp. Show examples that illustrate the different factors that affect a stamp's value.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Stamp Collecting Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 37, p. 49)* • **Group Organizer:** Skill Demonstration Checklist
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** BSA Youth Protection & Buddy System (Req 4): Complete all Stamp Catalogs and Condition Grading discussions and demonstrations with Two-Deep Leadership and your patrol buddy.
- **Common Myth:** The price listed in a standard stamp catalog is the exact cash amount a dealer will pay you for that stamp.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Catalog values estimate retail selling prices for high-quality examples, and common stamps often sell for much less depending on condition and market demand (p. 37).

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- The Scott catalog value is a retail price that reflects recent selling prices (p. 37).
- Supply and Demand Factors: The value of stamps also depends on supply and demand (p. 37).
- Common stamps often sell for less than catalog value (p. 37).
- Values also rise and fall as conditions in the stamp market change (p. 37).

#### Req 4a: Using Standard Stamp Catalogs
**Official Requirement 4a:** Demonstrate the use of ONE standard catalog for several different stamp issues. Explain why catalog value can vary from the corresponding purchase price.

- **Common Myth:** Every copy of the same stamp issue has the exact same monetary value regardless of how it looks.

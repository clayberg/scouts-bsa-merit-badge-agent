## 2. Pre-Class Prerequisite Checklist ('Bring Proof to Class')

- [ ] **Req 7 (Prepare):** Do TWO of the following: (a) Design a stamp, cancellation, or cachet. (b) Visit a post office, stamp club, or stamp show with an experienced collector. Explain what you saw and learned. (c) Write a review of an interesting article from a stamp newspaper, magazine, book, or website (with your parent or guardian's permission). (d) Research and report on a famous stamp-related personality or the history behind a particular stamp. (e) Describe the steps taken to produce a stamp. Include the methods of printing, types of paper, perforation styles, and how they are gummed. (f) Prepare a two- to three-page display involving stamps. Using ingenuity, as well as clippings, drawings, etc., tell a story about the stamps and how they relate to history, geography, or a favorite topic of yours.

### Req 2: Topical and Specialized Collecting Fields
**Official Requirement 2:** Define topical stamp collecting. Name and describe three other types of stamp collections.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Stamp Collecting Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 20, p. 22)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 2 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** Why might a collector choose a thematic topic rather than a worldwide album?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Topical collections assemble stamps depicting specific flora, fauna, aviation, or sporting events.
  - Country Specialty: Geographic collections restrict scope to one postal authority and its territorial dependencies.
  - Postal history examines postal routes, rates, cancellations, and markings on authentic envelopes.
  - Aerophilately Branch: Airmail specialists collect dedicated air post adhesive issues, flight covers, and cachets.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Subject Definition: Select a specific subject such as space exploration or Scouting for topical research.
  - Step 2 - Checklist Assembly: Compile a comprehensive checklist of pertinent worldwide issues using specialized thematic catalogs.
  - Step 3 - Genre Classification: Group reference specimens into country collections, topical arrangements, and transit postal histories.
  - Step 4 - Narrative Structuring: Arrange thematic pages logically by chronological narrative rather than issuing nation sequence.
- **Common Myth:** Beginners often believe all collectors must collect every stamp issued by their native country.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Thematic collecting allows complete creative freedom to assemble worldwide issues around personal interests.

### Req 1: Climbing Hazards and First Aid
**Official Requirement 1 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Explain to your counselor the most likely hazards you may encounter while participating in climbing and rappelling activities and what you should do to anticipate, help prevent, mitigate, and respond to these hazards. (b) Show that you know first aid for and how to prevent injuries or illnesses that could occur during climbing activities, including heat and cold reactions, dehydration, stopped breathing, sprains, abrasions, fractures, rope burns, blisters, snakebite, concussions, and insect bites or stings. (c) Identify the conditions that must exist before performing CPR on a person.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Climbing Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 28, p. 30)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Follow BSA Guide to Safe Scouting field protocols for Climbing Requirement 1: travel with a buddy, carry the Scout Basic Essentials and water while studying climbing hazards and first aid, and respect wildlife distance and Leave No Trace guidelines.
- **Common Myth:** Many climbers assume dehydration is only a danger when climbing on hot, sunny summer days.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Dehydration can happen in cold or warm weather whenever a climber sweats heavily or fails to drink enough water throughout the day (p. 28).

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Dehydration, caused by lack of water in the body, can occur in cold or warm weather, anytime a person is sweating profusely and/or not drinking enough (p. 28).
- Preventing Fluid Loss: Avoid dehydration by drinking plenty of fluids and eating enough throughout the day to keep your body well-balanced (p. 28).
- Treating Heat Exhaustion: If you become weary or develop a headache or body aches, or if you become confused, rest in the shade and sip water until the symptoms subside (p. 28).
- Signs of Frostbite: If the freezing continues, the area will stiffen and become grayish-white in color (p. 28).

### Req 6: Climbing Rope Selection and Care
**Official Requirement 6 (Parent Overview):** Rope. Do the following: (a) Describe the kinds of rope acceptable for use in climbing and rappelling. **Source:** *Official Scouting America Climbing Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 42, p. 85)* • **Group Organizer:** Skill Demonstration Checklist

**Official Requirement 1 (Parent Overview):** Select three of the professions from Group 1 listed below which interest you, then complete the following: Group 1: (1) Allopathic physician (MD), all specialties (2) Osteopathic physician (DO), all specialties (3) Podiatrist (DPM) (4) Chiropractor (DC) (5) Nurse Practitioner (NP) (6) Psychologist (PhD or PsyD) (7) Optometrist (OD) (8) Audiologist (AudD) (a) Briefly describe to your counselor the roles these professionals play in the delivery of health care. (b) Describe to your counselor the educational and licensing requirements for the professionals you selected.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Health Care Professions Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 16, p. 17)* • **Group Organizer:** Career & Profession Exploration Card
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Conduct all counselor discussions, community interviews, and research for Health Care Professions Requirement 1 on primary diagnosing and prescribing clinicians under BSA Youth Protection two-deep leadership, copying a parent or guardian on any external correspondence.
- **Common Myth:** Osteopathic physicians (DOs) are only allowed to perform spinal manipulation and cannot prescribe medications or perform surgery like allopathic physicians (MDs).
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Both MDs and DOs are fully licensed physicians who complete four years of medical school plus postgraduate residency to diagnose illnesses, prescribe medications, and perform surgery in any specialty (p. 16).

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Multidisciplinary Care Delivery: Physicians and Physician Assistants PHYSICIANS and PHYSICIAN ASSISTANTS Many different professionals play a part in the delivery of healthcare (p. 16).
- State Licensing Law Variations: As these laws vary somewhat around the country, information on licensing for each profession cannot be included here (p. 16).
- State Licensing Board Resources: Such information can usually be obtained from your state'sprofessional licensing board or the equivalent (p. 16).
- Allopathic Medical School Training: This professional is trained in the practice of medicine and surgery at a medical school granting a Doctor of Medicine (MD) degree (p. 16).

- **Common Myth:** Orthotists and prosthetists simply order pre-made braces from a catalog while physical therapists build the actual artificial limbs.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**

- Orthotist and Prosthetist Supervision: They most likely would work under the supervision of an orthotist or prosthetist who would design the artificial limb, brace or removable splint (p. 47).
- These professionals perform imaging of patients using a variety of techniques (p. 47).

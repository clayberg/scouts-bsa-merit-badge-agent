### Req 7: Patient Confidentiality and HIPAA

**Source:** *Official Scouting America Health Care Professions Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 12, p. 14)* • **Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 7 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix (Role of Confidentiality Between A)
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Never discuss patient information in public hospital areas such as cafeterias, elevators, or waiting rooms.

- **Pamphlet Fact:** HIPAA prohibits sharing identifying clinical details, including room numbers, diagnoses, or dates that could identify patients.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Honest disclosure occurs only when patients trust that their private medical information remains protected.
- Federal HIPAA Mandates: The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act establishes national rules safeguarding protected patient records.

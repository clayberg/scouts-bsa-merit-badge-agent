  - Rescuers confirm unresponsiveness and absence of normal breathing before initiating chest compressions on a dry flat surface. Automated external defibrillators require drying the victim's chest completely before pad placement.

- **Pamphlet Fact:** Immediate chest compressions take absolute priority because seawater enters lungs in small frothy volumes.

### Req 2: Swimming Merit Badge Prerequisite
**Official Requirement 2:** Before completing requirements 3 through 6, earn the Swimming merit badge.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Scuba Diving Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 9, p. 17)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 2 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Do not attempt open water scuba training if basic surface swimming proficiency cannot be maintained.
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** Why does surface swimming efficiency directly influence gas consumption during a scuba dive?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Strong swimming strokes ensure divers navigate surface currents without exhausting their breathing gas.
  - Survival Floating: Resting strokes permit calm recovery when waiting for boat pickup in choppy waters.
  - Treading Technique: Effective rotary kicks maintain clear airway positioning above waves while conserving physical energy.
  - Subsurface Comfort: Breath holding drills instill confidence during accidental regulator displacement or mask clearing incidents.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Record Verification: Review personal advancement tracking records to confirm successful Swimming merit badge completion documentation.
  - Step 2 - Stroke Evaluation: Demonstrate strong forward propulsion using efficient crawl and breaststroke techniques in deep water.
  - Step 3 - Water Tread Test: Maintain head clearance above water continuously for ten minutes using gentle leg kicks.
  - Step 4 - Surface Assessment: Complete an unassisted continuous swim across two hundred meters without resting on walls.
- **Common Myth:** Students consider a basic dog paddle sufficient swimming competence for heavy scuba open water training.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Scuba training organizations require continuous unassisted distance swimming and unequipped survival water treading tests.

### Req 3: Scuba Diver Code Guidelines
**Official Requirement 3:** Discuss the Scuba Diver's Code with your counselor, and explain the importance of each guideline to a scuba diver's safety.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Scuba Diving Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 19, p. 50)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 3 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Any diver can cancel or terminate a dive at any time without fear of criticism.
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** Why does personal accountability remain the foundational pillar of recreational diver safety systems?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Buddy Integrity: Paired divers maintain visual contact and agree upon underwater signals before water entry.
  - Equipment Readiness: Routine functional checks confirm working submersible pressure gauges and primary regulator stage delivery.
  - Environmental Protection: Buoyancy control prevents accidental fin contact against fragile coral heads and bottom silt.
  - Conservative Limits: Responsible divers plan bottom times well within designated recreational safe decompression limit tables.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Code Review: Read each behavioral guideline within the diver code alongside your designated merit counselor.
  - Step 2 - Risk Explanation: Analyze how refusing alcohol before diving prevents impaired judgment and dangerous underwater accidents.
  - Step 3 - Environmental Analysis: Discuss methods for securing dangling equipment gauges to prevent physical damage to reefs.
  - Step 4 - Commitment Signing: Pledge personal adherence to conservative ascent profiles and mandatory buddy checks before splashdown.
- **Common Myth:** Divers assume the code functions merely as an informal suggestion rather than safety protocol.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Adherence to the diver code directly prevents panic, equipment failure, and fatal out of air situations.

### Req 4: Open Water Diver Certification
**Official Requirement 4:** Earn an Open Water Diver Certification from a scuba organization recognized by the Scouting America scuba policy.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Scuba Diving Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 10, p. 12)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 4 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Breathe continuously and never hold your breath while breathing compressed gas underwater.
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** Why does Boyle's law dictate that the greatest relative pressure changes occur near the surface?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Regulator Recovery: Sweeping the right arm locates a dislodged hose for rapid underwater regulator replacement.
  - Mask Clearing: Tilting the head upward while exhaling through the nose purges flooded mask water.
  - Buoyancy Mastery: Adjusting low pressure inflator bursts achieves neutral hovering without sculling arms or legs.
  - Emergency Ascent: Humming continuously during an alternate air source ascent prevents pulmonary barotrauma lung injuries.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Classroom Instruction: Complete all academic knowledge modules covering dive tables, physics, physiology, and equipment mechanics.
  - Step 2 - Confined Water: Practice regulator clearing, buoyancy control, and air sharing drills inside a swimming pool.
  - Step 3 - Open Water: Execute four supervised ocean or lake checkout dives demonstrating core autonomous diving competencies.
  - Step 4 - Certification Filing: Submit validated logbook entries and instructor signed paperwork to secure official certification cards.
- **Common Myth:** Beginners think holding their breath during ascent helps conserve precious remaining cylinder air volume.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Expanding air in ascending lungs causes fatal alveolar rupture unless breathing normally at all times.

### Req 5: Aquatic Ecosystems and Habitats
**Official Requirement 5:** Explain what an ecosystem is, and describe four aquatic ecosystems a diver might experience.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Scuba Diving Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 73, p. 75)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 5 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Maintain horizontal trim and fin control to prevent silting or damaging fragile benthic communities.
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** In what specific ways do abiotic factors like sunlight penetration and water salinity shape marine ecological niches?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Coral Ecosystem: Tropical reefs feature calcium carbonate structures hosting symbiotic algae and diverse fish species.
  - Temperate giant kelp columns create vertical canopy shelter for marine mammals and invertebrates.
  - Freshwater Springs: Subterranean limestone aquifers produce crystal clear water supporting submerged grasses and freshwater turtles.
  - Rocky Reefs: Benthic boulders provide anchorage for colorful sea anemones, sponges, and bottom dwelling crustaceans.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Ecosystem Definition: Define an ecosystem by detailing interactions between living organisms and nonliving environmental components.
  - Step 2 - Coral Description: Characterize warm tropical coral reefs highlighting primary producer dynamics and high biodiversity balances.
  - Step 3 - Temperate Exploration: Detail cold water kelp forests and rocky reefs including nutrient rich upwelling systems.
  - Step 4 - Freshwater Examination: Explain inland limestone spring hydrology noting seasonal temperature stability and specialized cavern fauna.
- **Common Myth:** People believe coral formations are inanimate rocks that tolerate diver physical handling without consequence.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Corals are fragile living animal colonies that perish when touched or kicked by careless fins.

### Req 6: Professional Diving Career Opportunities
**Official Requirement 6:** Find out about three career opportunities in the scuba industry. Pick one and find out the education, training, and experience required for this profession. Discuss this with your counselor, and explain why this profession might interest you.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Scuba Diving Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 83, p. 86)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Career & Profession Exploration Card):** Req 6 Career & Professional Pathway Investigation Matrix
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Commercial and scientific diving requires adherence to strict institutional dive safety manual protocols.
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** In what specific ways do the equipment requirements for surface supplied commercial diving differ from recreational scuba?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Commercial Operations: Surface supplied diving contractors execute deep underwater welding, salvage, and offshore rig construction.
  - Marine scientists use scientific diving methods to collect biological specimens and environmental samples.
  - Instructional Leadership: Certified instructors teach ocean stewardship, equipment maintenance, and autonomous open water diver safety.
  - Public Safety: Police and rescue dive teams recover submerged evidence and conduct aquatic victim searches.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Industry Research: Investigate commercial diving, scientific research diving, and recreational instruction career tracks thoroughly online.
  - Step 2 - Career Selection: Select one specialized diving profession and list all required collegiate or vocational credentials.
  - Step 3 - Prerequisite Mapping: Document necessary logged open water dives, medical fitness clearances, and specialized technical certifications.
  - Step 4 - Counselor Discussion: Present findings regarding training requirements and explain personal motivations for entering this field.
- **Common Myth:** Candidates think recreational open water certification qualifies a diver for commercial offshore construction jobs.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Commercial divers attend accredited diving academies to master surface supplied helmets and underwater burning equipment.

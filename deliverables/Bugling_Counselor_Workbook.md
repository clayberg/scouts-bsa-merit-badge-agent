**Source:** *Official Scouting America Bugling Merit Badge Pamphlet* • **Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 1 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix (History and Origins of the Bugle)
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** BSA Youth Protection & Buddy System (Req 1): Complete all History and Origins of the Bugle discussions and demonstrations with Two-Deep Leadership and your patrol buddy.

### Req 2: Brass Acoustics and Call Composition
**Official Requirement 2 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Explain and demonstrate how the bugle makes sound, and explain how the bugle is related to other brass wind instruments. (b) Compose a bugle call for your troop or patrol to signal a common group activity, such as assembling for mealtime or striking a campsite. Play the call that you have composed before your unit or patrol.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Bugling Merit Badge Pamphlet* • **Group Organizer:** Skill Demonstration Checklist
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** BSA Youth Protection & Buddy System (Req 2): Complete all Brass Acoustics and Call Composition discussions and demonstrations with Two-Deep Leadership and your patrol buddy.
- **Common Myth:** Blowing air directly into a bugle mouthpiece produces sound the same way blowing across a whistle does.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** A bugle produces sound only when the player buzzes their lips against the mouthpiece, creating vibrations amplified by the brass tubing (p. 12).

#### Req 2a: Bugle Acoustics and Brass Family
**Official Requirement 2a:** Explain and demonstrate how the bugle makes sound, and explain how the bugle is related to other brass wind instruments.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Bugling Merit Badge Pamphlet* • **Organizer (Skill Demonstration Checklist):** Req 2a Hands-On Skill Station & EDGE Verification Checklist (Bugle Makes Sound)
- **Common Myth:** Because a standard regulation bugle has no valves, it can only play two or three different notes.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** By adjusting lip tension and air speed, a bugler can play five or six clear open notes along the instrument's natural harmonic series (p. 12).

**Official Requirement 2b:** Compose a bugle call for your troop or patrol to signal a common group activity, such as assembling for mealtime or striking a campsite. Play the call that you have composed before your unit or patrol.

### Req 5: Bugle Care, Cleaning, and Maintenance
**Official Requirement 5:** Explain how to care for, clean, and maintain a bugle.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Bugling Merit Badge Pamphlet* • **Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 5 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix (Bugle Care, Cleaning, and Maintenance)
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** BSA Youth Protection & Buddy System (Req 5): Complete all Bugle Care, Cleaning, and Maintenance discussions and demonstrations with Two-Deep Leadership and your patrol buddy.
- **Common Myth:** Boiling hot water and abrasive kitchen cleansers are the best way to sterilize the inside of a brass bugle.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Hot water and harsh abrasives strip protective lacquer and damage brass, so bugles must be washed only in lukewarm water with mild dish soap (p. 22).

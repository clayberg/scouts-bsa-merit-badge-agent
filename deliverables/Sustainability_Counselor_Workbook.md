**Official Requirement 1:** Describe the meaning of sustainability in your own words. Explain the importance of sustainability to society and how you can contribute to fulfilling the needs of current generations without compromising the needs of future generations.

- **Common Myth:** Sustainability focuses solely on protecting wilderness areas from any human resource extraction.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Achieving true sustainability requires balancing environmental protection with social and economic needs so both current and future generations can thrive (p. 15).

### Req 2: Household Water Use and Supply
**Official Requirement 2 (Parent Overview):** Water. Do ONE of the following and discuss with your counselor: (a) Evaluate your household water usage. If available, review water bills from the past year and evaluate the seasonal changes in water use. Identify three ways to help reduce water consumption. (b) Explain why water is necessary in our lives. Create a diagram to show how your household gets its clean water from a natural source and what happens with the water after you use it. Tell two ways to preserve your community's access to clean water in the future. (c) Different areas of the world are affected by either too much (flooding) or too little (drought) water. Explore whether either or both affect where you live. Identify three water conservation or flood mitigation practices (successful or unsuccessful) that have been tried where you live or in an area of the world that interests you.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Sustainability Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 29, p. 32)* • **Group Organizer:** Field Sketch & Diagram Canvas
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** BSA Youth Protection & Buddy System (Req 2): Complete all Household Water Use and Supply discussions and demonstrations with Two-Deep Leadership and your patrol buddy.
- **Common Myth:** Flooding always develops gradually over several days of heavy rain, giving communities plenty of time to evacuate.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** While some floods develop slowly, flash floods can strike with no warning at all and cause immediate danger (p. 29).

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Flooding is a temporary overflow of water onto normally dryland (p. 29).
- It's the most common natural disaster in the United States (p. 29).
- Causes of Severe Flooding: Floods result from rain, snow, coastal storms, storms urge, and overflows of dams and other water systems (p. 29).
- They can develop slowly or quickly; flash floods can come with no warning at all (p. 29).

- **Pamphlet Fact:** Small appliances and phone chargers continue drawing standby power when plugged in, so unplugging them when not in use saves energy (p. 62).

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Close window drapes to keep heat out in summer, and open the drapes to letin the warm sunin wintertime (p. 62).
- Shrinking Your Carbon Footprint: By using less fuel and less electricity and reducing the amount of trash you create, you can reduce your carbon footprint dramatically (p. 62).
- Powering Down Unused Electronics: Start by turning off computers, televisions, and lights when you are not using them (p. 62).
- Unplug small appliances and phone chargers, etc., when they are not in use (p. 62).

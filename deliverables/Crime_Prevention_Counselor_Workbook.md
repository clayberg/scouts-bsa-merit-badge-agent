### Req 1: Jurisprudence and Crime Prevention Principles
**Official Requirement 1 (Parent Overview):** Laws and Society. Discuss the following with your counselor: (a) Why we have criminal laws (b) The types of crimes, including property crimes, crimes against people, white collar crime, and environmental crime (c) Why people commit crimes (d) Why everyone should follow the law even when no one is watching (e) The meaning of crime prevention
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Crime Prevention Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 11, p. 15)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Never confront active statutory offenders or intervene directly during commission of violent or property offenses.
- **Common Myth:** Criminal statutes exist solely to punish wrongdoers after illegal actions inflict harm on innocent victims.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Statutory penal codes establish behavioral boundaries that protect individual liberties and maintain civil stability across communities.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Social Contract Principle: Criminal statutes preserve orderly civilization by defining clear behavioral standards for community safety.
- Classification Of Offenses: Legal codes divide statutory violations into property offenses, violent acts, and regulatory crimes.
- Removing criminal opportunity diminishes overall offense volume regardless of underlying social motives.
- Civic Moral Duty: Voluntary obedience to legal statutes protects community welfare even without police presence.

### Req 5: Commercial Retail Loss Prevention
**Official Requirement 5 (Parent Overview):** Retail Crime Prevention. Research the following topics and review them with your counselor: (a) The impact of shoplifting and employee theft (also known as shrinkage) and loss prevention on retail finances, customer service, and reputation (b) Techniques used by retail stores to prevent shoplifting
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Crime Prevention Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 7, p. 54)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Never attempt to physically detain, pursue, or confront suspected retail shoplifters inside or outside stores.
- **Common Myth:** Shoplifting constitutes a minor victimless offense because corporate commercial retail insurance reimburses all inventory losses.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Retail shrinkage encompasses internal employee theft, organized retail crime, vendor fraud, and amateur shoplifting losses.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Retail Shrinkage Economics: Unchecked store inventory losses drive consumer price increases and eliminate entry level retail employment.
- Electronic Article Surveillance: Radio frequency and acousto-magnetic tags trigger portal alarms when unpaid inventory passes exit gates.
- Cash Handling Controls: Register audits and dual authorization procedures deter internal associate theft during point-of-sale transactions.
- Attentive Customer Greeting: Proactive employee engagement creates natural surveillance that deters shoplifters from attempting merchandise concealment.

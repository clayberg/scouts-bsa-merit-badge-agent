## 2. Pre-Class Prerequisite Checklist ('Bring Proof to Class')

- [ ] **Req 3 (Explain):** Crime in Your Community, State, and Nation. Do the following: (a) With your parent or guardian's permission and the approval of your counselor, research local, state, or national news coverage of three crimes of different types. Research how common these types of crimes are in your state or in the United States. (b) Record notes on which law enforcement agencies and courts were involved in the pursuit of justice for the victims and the accused person, why you think these crimes were committed, and what could be done to prevent similar crimes. Review your research with your counselor.
- [ ] **Req 10 (Discuss):** Interview. With your parent or guardian's permission and your counselor's approval, interview a law enforcement officer or a civil servant about their work in crime prevention. Learn about how they chose this career and about their duties. Discuss what you learned with your counselor.

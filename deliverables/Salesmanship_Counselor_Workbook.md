## 2. Pre-Class Prerequisite Checklist ('Bring Proof to Class')

- [ ] **Req 2 (Explain):** Explain why it is important for a salesperson to do the following: (a) Research the market to be sure the product or service meets the needs of customers. (b) Learn all about the product to be sold. (c) If possible, visit the location where the product is built and learn how it is constructed. If a service is being sold, learn about the benefits of the service to the customer. (d) Follow up with customers after their purchase to confirm their satisfaction and discuss their concerns about the product.
- [ ] **Req 5 (Prepare):** Do ONE of the following and keep a record (cost sheet). Use the sales techniques you have learned, and share your experience with your counselor: (a) Help your unit raise funds through sales of merchandise or of tickets to a Scout event. (b) Sell your services, such as lawn raking or mowing, pet sitting, dog walking, snow shoveling, or car washing, to your neighbors. Follow up after completing the service to determine the customer satisfaction. (c) Earn money through retail selling.
- [ ] **Req 6 (Discuss):** Do ONE of the following: (a) Interview a salesperson and learn the following: (1) What made the person choose sales as a profession? (2) What are the most important things to remember when talking to customers? (3) How is the product sold? (4) Include your own questions. (b) Interview a retail store owner and learn the following: (1) How often is the owner approached by a sales representative? (2) What good traits should a sales representative have? What habits should the sales representative avoid? (3) What does the owner consider when deciding whether to establish an account with a sales representative? (4) Include at least two of your own questions.

### Req 4: Product Presentation and Demonstration Execution
**Official Requirement 4:** Make a sales presentation of a product assigned by your counselor.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Salesmanship Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 26, p. 28)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Skill Demonstration Checklist):** Req 4 Hands-On Skill Station & EDGE Verification Checklist
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** In what specific ways do open-ended questions help you tailor a product demonstration to a buyer's exact situation?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Opening statements should highlight common customer operational challenges rather than direct pitching.
  - Interactive Feature Demonstration: Allowing prospective buyers to operate sample merchandise builds immediate personal ownership interest.
  - Constructive Objection Handling: Rephrasing customer pricing hesitations acknowledges genuine concerns while clarifying long-term durability value.
  - Trial Close Technique: Asking confirmation questions throughout the demonstration measures customer readiness to purchase merchandise.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Needs Discovery Interview: Ask targeted operational questions to uncover specific buyer pain points before presenting.
  - Step 2 - Core Benefit Demonstration: Display key merchandise features that directly solve the identified buyer operational difficulties.
  - Step 3 - Value Based Objection Answering: Address price resistance by contrasting initial investment expense against extended equipment lifespan.
  - Step 4 - Agreement Request Closing: Propose a clear order quantity and invite the prospective customer to finalize purchase terms.
- **Common Myth:** A successful presentation is a memorized monologue delivered without interruption from the buyer.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** The best sales presentations are two-way conversations that allow prospects to ask questions and handle products directly.

### Req 3: Wood Fibers and Pulping Processes
**Official Requirement 3 (Parent Overview):** Name at least four types of trees that are major sources of papermaking fibers. Then do the following: (a) Discuss what other uses are made of the trees and the forestland owned by the pulp and paper industry. (b) Describe two ways of getting fibers from wood, and explain the major differences between them. (c) Tell why some pulps are bleached, and describe the process.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Pulp and Paper Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 6)* • **Group Organizer:** 2-Column Comparison Table
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** BSA Youth Protection & Buddy System (Req 3): Complete all Wood Fibers and Pulping Processes discussions and demonstrations with Two-Deep Leadership and your patrol buddy.
- **Common Myth:** Softwood and hardwood trees produce identical cellulose fibers that can be swapped without changing how a sheet of paper performs.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Coniferous softwoods like pine and spruce have long fibers that provide tear strength, whereas deciduous hardwoods like oak and birch have shorter fibers that create a smooth printing surface (p. 18).

#### Req 3a: Multiple Forestland and Timber Uses
**Official Requirement 3a:** Discuss what other uses are made of the trees and the forestland owned by the pulp and paper industry.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Pulp and Paper Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 6)* • **Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 3a Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix (Discuss What Other Uses Are)
- **Common Myth:** Mechanical pulping produces stronger, longer-lasting paper than chemical pulping because it keeps all parts of the wood together.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Mechanical grinding damages fibers and leaves natural lignin in the pulp, which makes newsprint weaker and causes it to turn yellow and brittle when exposed to sunlight (p. 20).

#### Req 3b: Mechanical Versus Chemical Pulping
**Official Requirement 3b:** Describe two ways of getting fibers from wood, and explain the major differences between them.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Pulp and Paper Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 6)* • **Organizer (2-Column Comparison Table):** Req 3b Technical Comparison Matrix (Getting Fibers From Wood)

### Req 5: Wood Species Selection Criteria
**Official Requirement 5:** Tell why different woods are used for different projects. Explain why you chose the type of wood you did for your projects in requirements 6 and 7.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Wood Carving Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 29, p. 43)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 5 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Wear dust masks when sanding exotic or domestic wood blanks to avoid respiratory irritation.
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** Why is basswood widely considered the premier wood for instructional hand carving projects?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - American basswood features tight uniform grain that cuts easily in any direction cleanly.
  - Sugar pine and eastern white pine carve smoothly but splinter if knots appear.
  - Hardwood Density: Walnut and cherry hold crisp carved detail but require sharp tools and mallet strikes.
  - End grain surfaces absorb knife strokes heavily and require shallow angled paring passes.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Species Comparison: Examine sample blocks of basswood, pine, butternut, and walnut for grain spacing.
  - Step 2 - Defect Inspection: Check the wooden blank carefully for knots, surface cracks, and irregular sapwood.
  - Step 3 - Grain Orientation: Orient the workpiece so main contour lines flow parallel to wood grain fibers.

### Req 7: Relief and Chip Carving
**Official Requirement 7:** Complete a simple low-relief OR a chip carving project.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Wood Carving Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 55, p. 56)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 7 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Always clamp relief boards to the workbench so both hands remain behind the cutting chisel.
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** Why must relief carving blanks be clamped rather than held in the carver's hand?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Relief Grounding: Level recessed background areas evenly using flat dogleg chisels or wide sweep gouges.
  - Three corner chip carving patterns require three angled incisions meeting at one point.
  - Transfer geometric layouts accurately onto surfaced wood blanks using carbon tracing paper sheets.
  - Undercut Shadow: Undercutting perimeter edges of relief figures creates dramatic contrast against the flat background.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Board Clamping: Secure the flat carving board firmly to the benchtop using padded wooden clamps.
  - Step 2 - Outline Scoring: Cut vertical stop incisions along all drawn pattern outlines with a skew chisel.
  - Step 3 - Waste Extraction: Slice waste chips away from design borders toward scored stop cut lines cleanly.
  - Step 4 - Surface Leveling: Flatten the recessed background plane smoothly using a bent gouge or hand scraper.
- **Common Myth:** Chip carving chips can be pried out using the knife tip if cuts do not meet.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Prying with blade tips snaps delicate knife steel and tears surrounding wood fibers permanently.

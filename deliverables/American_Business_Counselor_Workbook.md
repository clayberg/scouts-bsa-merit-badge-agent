- **Pamphlet Fact:** Economic freedom carries the responsibility to respect others' rights and follow the law, whereas license is the abuse of freedom without regard for ethics or harm to others (p. 19).

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**

- Second Industrial Revolution Leadership: By the end of the century, the United States became the world leader in manufacturing and spurred what is known as the Second Industrial Revolution (p. 19).

### Req 1: Foundational Artificial Intelligence Terminology
**Official Requirement 1:** Key Concepts. Define the following terms and share the meaning of each with your counselor: artificial intelligence (AI), artificial intelligence agents, automation, basic programming, bots, data, databases, digital workers, general AI, machine learning (ML), narrow AI, superintelligent AI, tasks, triggers, workflows, and variables.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Artificial Intelligence Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 6, p. 7)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 1 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** How does a deterministic software program differ fundamentally from a probabilistic machine learning agent?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - System Classification: Artificial intelligence systems process external inputs to execute rational actions toward defined objectives.
  - Autonomous Behavior: Intelligent agents perceive dynamic operational environments using integrated sensor arrays to choose actions.
  - Structured Storage: Relational databases organize enterprise data collections to support high-throughput machine learning inference pipelines.
  - Algorithmic Routine: Basic programming establishes deterministic sequential logic routines executing instructions within computer hardware systems.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Vocabulary Cataloging: Record formal definitions for core intelligence and automation terminology in your notebook.
  - Step 2 - Comparative Mapping: Contrast deterministic programmatic logic against probabilistic machine learning models on a chart.
  - Step 3 - Architecture Diagramming: Sketch the structural operational pipeline connecting raw relational data stores to autonomous agents.
  - Step 4 - Counselor Demonstration: Present your glossary terms directly to your counselor using concrete computational examples.
- **Common Myth:** Artificial intelligence implies that computer hardware possesses genuine biological consciousness and emotional self-awareness.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Artificial intelligence encompasses computational systems that simulate cognitive functions through probabilistic models and structured algorithms.

### Req 5: Comprehensive Business Plan Architecture
**Official Requirement 5 (Parent Overview):** Create a written business plan for your idea that includes all of the following: (a) Product or Service (1) Describe the product or service to be offered. (2) Identify goals for your business. (3) Explain how you can make enough of the product or perform the service to meet your goals. (4) Identify and describe the potential liability risks for your product or service. (5) Determine what type of license, if any, you might need in order to sell or make your product or service. (b) Market Analysis (1) Identify the types of people who would buy your product or service. (2) Identify your business's competitors, and describe their strengths and weaknesses. (3) Describe what makes your product or service unique. (c) Financial (1) Determine how much money you will need to start your business, and identify how you will obtain the money. (2) Determine the cost of offering your product or service and the price you will charge in order to make a profit. (3) Describe what will happen with the money you make from the sales of your product or service. (d) Personnel (1) Determine what parts of the business you will handle yourself, and describe your qualifications. (2) Determine whether you will need additional help to operate your business. If you will need help, describe the responsibilities and qualifications needed for the personnel who will fill each role. (e) Promotion and Marketing (1) Describe the methods you will use to promote your business to potential customers. (2) Explain how you will utilize the internet and social media to increase awareness of your product or service. (3) Design a promotional flyer or poster for your product or service.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Entrepreneurship Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 54, p. 55)* • **Group Organizer:** Skill Demonstration Checklist
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Include standard personal protective equipment protocols within all physical production and service delivery plans.
- **Common Myth:** Business plans are purely decorative documents created solely to satisfy lending bank paperwork.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** A thorough business plan guides daily resource allocation and exposes fatal operational flaws early.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Written Plan Foundation: Documenting operational strategies establishes accountability and clarifies financial goals for prospective investors.
- Integrated Structural Components: Combining marketing, finance, and operations prevents isolated functional failures within new ventures.
- Milestone Roadmap Scheduling: Realistic production timelines ensure customer orders match physical capacity without inventory spoilage.
- Risk Mitigation Planning: Formulating contingency budgets buffers unexpected equipment breakdowns during initial commercial launch weeks.

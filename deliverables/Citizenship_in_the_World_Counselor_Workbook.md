**Official Requirement 3 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Pick a current world event. - Resource Allocation: Geographic distribution of petroleum reserves and arable land dictates regional power relationships.
- Climate Influence: Severe weather systems and agricultural shifts force neighboring states into collaborative environmental pacts.

### Req 5: Comparative Systems of Government
**Official Requirement 5 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Discuss the differences between constitutional and nonconstitutional governments. (b) Name at least five different types of governments currently in power in the world. (c) Show on a world map countries that use each of these five different forms of government.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Citizenship in the World Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 23, p. 25)* • **Group Organizer:** 2-Column Comparison Table
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Exercise diplomatic courtesy and objective academic vocabulary when analyzing controversial foreign political structures.
- **Common Myth:** Every country that writes and adopts a constitution operates as a genuine constitutional democracy.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Authoritarian states often write constitutions while completely ignoring legal limitations on executive state power.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Constitutional Checks: Written constitutions distribute governance powers among distinct branches to prevent tyrannical executive overreach.
- Authoritarian Rule: Nonconstitutional regimes centralize absolute control within ruling cliques that suppress independent political parties.
- In parliamentary systems, executive ministers are chosen directly by elected members of parliament.
- Hereditary monarchs in absolute kingdoms retain unrestricted authority over legal statutes and treasury funds.

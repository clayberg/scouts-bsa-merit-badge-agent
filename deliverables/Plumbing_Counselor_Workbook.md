### Req 1: Home Sanitation and Health Codes
**Official Requirement 1 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Describe how a properly working plumbing system protects your family's health and safety. (b) List five important local health regulations related to plumbing and tell how they protect health and safety. (c) Describe the safety precautions you must take when making home plumbing repairs.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Plumbing Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 20, p. 40)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Always wear safety glasses and chemical-resistant gloves when handling drain piping containing biological pathogens.
- **Common Myth:** Drain traps exist solely to catch dropped jewelry before it washes into the sewer.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Fixture traps maintain a water seal that blocks dangerous pathogens and lethal sewer gases.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Potable Supply Isolation: Pressurized incoming pipes deliver clean water while preventing contaminated waste water from mingling.
- Standing water in fixture traps seals out toxic flammable methane sewer gas.
- Backflow Prevention Device: Atmospheric vacuum breakers prevent backsiphonage of dirty fluids into municipal supply lines.
- Vertical roof vent stacks balance atmospheric pressure to keep drain lines flowing.

### Req 2: Supply and Drainage System Layout
**Official Requirement 2 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Make a drawing and explain how a home hot- and cold-water supply system works. Tell how you would make it safe from freezing. (b) Make a drawing and explain the drainage system of the plumbing in a house. Show and explain the use of drains and vents.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Plumbing Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 9, p. 14)* • **Group Organizer:** Field Sketch & Diagram Canvas
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Ensure water heaters are completely de-energized at the breaker box before draining hot water plumbing lines.
- **Common Myth:** Drain pipes work best when installed completely horizontal to keep wastewater moving slowly.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Drain pipes require a downward slope of one-quarter inch per foot to maintain scouring velocity.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Municipal or well pressure forces fresh water through rigid trunk and branch distribution lines.
- Freeze Protection Drainage: Exterior sillcocks require frost-proof valves and interior shutoffs with manual bleeder drain caps.
- Drainage Gravity Fall: Horizontal drain pipes must maintain one quarter inch per foot downward pitch.
- Open roof vent pipes admit atmospheric air to prevent siphoning of fixture traps.

### Req 6: Practical Plumbing Assembly Skills
**Official Requirement 6 (Parent Overview):** Do FOUR of the following, each under the supervision of a knowledgeable adult: (a) Visit the plumbing section of a hardware store or home center and identify: (1) PVC pipe and fittings (2) CPVC pipe and fittings (3) PEX pipe and fittings (4) Copper pipe and fittings (5) Steel pipe and fittings (6) Specialty plumbing tools (b) Cut, clean (deburr), PVC or CPVC pipe; solvent weld at least three connections to include a coupling, tee, and elbow. (c) Cut PEX pipe; make at least one connection using either a quick-connect fitting or a crimp fitting (using specialized tools). (d) Solder a copper connection using a gas torch. (e) Replace a kitchen or lavatory faucet. (f) Remove, clean or replace, and reinstall a sink or lavatory drain trap. (g) Properly apply pipe thread tape to a pipe or a plumbing connector.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Plumbing Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 15, p. 36)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Keep a certified fire extinguisher and heat shield pad directly behind copper joints when operating torches.
- **Common Myth:** Applying extra layers of pipe thread tape guarantees a tighter seal on mechanical compression fittings.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Thread tape lubricates tapered pipe threads and will ruin the seal on flat washer compression joints.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Pipe ends must be cut exactly square to seat fully inside fitting sockets.
- Primer softens plastic surfaces while solvent cement chemically welds fitting and pipe together.
- Capillary Action Soldering: Molten solder flows inward toward heat to seal clean fluxed copper joint gaps.
- Thread Sealant Direction: Wrap PTFE tape clockwise around male pipe threads to prevent unravelling during assembly.

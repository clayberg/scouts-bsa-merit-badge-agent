### Req 1: Plumbing Health Codes and Repair Safety
**Official Requirement 1 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Describe how a properly working plumbing system protects your family's health and safety. (b) List five important local health regulations related to plumbing and tell how they protect health and safety. (c) Describe the safety precautions you must take when making home plumbing repairs.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Plumbing Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 20, p. 40)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** BSA Youth Protection & Buddy System (Req 1): Complete all Plumbing Health Codes and Repair Safety discussions and demonstrations with Two-Deep Leadership and your patrol buddy.
- **Common Myth:** Plumbing codes exist mainly to make home repairs more complicated and require homeowners to hire licensed contractors for every minor fix.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Plumbing regulations are essential health safeguards designed to keep potable drinking water completely separated from contaminated wastewater (p. 20).

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Household Pipe Network: Plumbing systems carry water into and out of a home through a system of pipes (p. 20).
- Clean Water Supply: For the health and safety of the people who live there, the water entering the home through the water-supply system must be pure and clean (p. 20).
- Wastewater should never come into contact with the water that the family uses for drinking, bathing, or washing clothes and dishes (p. 20).
- Routine System Inspection: This is the main reason that it is essential that your family's plumbing system be properly installed and regularly checked to make sure it is in good (p. 20).

### Req 2: Water Supply and Drainage Vent Systems
**Official Requirement 2 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Make a drawing and explain how a home hot- and cold-water supply system works. Tell how you would make it safe from freezing. (b) Make a drawing and explain the drainage system of the plumbing in a house. Show and explain the use of drains and vents.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Plumbing Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 9, p. 14)* • **Group Organizer:** Field Sketch & Diagram Canvas
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** BSA Youth Protection & Buddy System (Req 2): Complete all Water Supply and Drainage Vent Systems discussions and demonstrations with Two-Deep Leadership and your patrol buddy.
- **Common Myth:** Household wastewater drains work solely because water pressure from the street main pushes waste through the sewer pipes.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** While clean supply water enters the home under pressure, the drainage system relies on gravity and atmospheric air vents to carry waste away smoothly (p. 9).

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Drain Waste Vent System: The air vents are an important part of waste disposal, which is why the drainage system is often called the drain-waste-vent (DWV) system (p. 9).
- The drain and vent pipes are tied together, or share pipes, and use the same types of pipe (p. 9).
- Soil Pipe Vent Extension: As you can see in the illustration showing the drainage and supply system, the main vent is an extension of the soil pipe (p. 9).
- Without air vents, the plumbing system would becomeair-bound, like a small-necked bottle filled with water (p. 9).

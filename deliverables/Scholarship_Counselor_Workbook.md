### Req 1: Academic Achievement and Study Habits
**Official Requirement 1 (Parent Overview):** Do ONE of the following: (a) Show that you have had an average grade of B or higher (80% or higher) for one term or semester. (b) Show that for one term or semester you have improved your school grades over the previous period.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Scholarship Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 19, p. 21)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** BSA Youth Protection & Buddy System (Req 1): Complete all Academic Achievement and Study Habits discussions and demonstrations with Two-Deep Leadership and your patrol buddy.
- **Common Myth:** Only straight-A students who already have an 80 percent average or higher can earn the Scholarship merit badge.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Scouts can fulfill Requirement 1 either by earning a B average or higher for a term or by showing grade improvement over the previous period (p. 19).

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- For requirement 1, you must show that you earned an average grade of Bor higher for one term, or that you have improved your grades over the previous (p. 19).
- Find a place at home where you feel comfortable and where you can focus on the task at hand (p. 19).
- The place you select should be well-lighted and relatively quiet at the time of day you will be doing your homework (p. 19).
- Sturdy Desk and Chair Setup: It also should have a sturdy chair and a table or desk where you can spread out your papers and books (p. 19).

- Building a Well-Oiled Team: The more your troop works together, the more well-oiled the teamwork machine becomes (p. 52).

### Req 5: Lifelong Learning and Career Reports
**Official Requirement 5 (Parent Overview):** Do ONE of the following: (a) Write a report of 250 to 300 words about how the education you receive in school will be of value to you in the future and how you will continue to educate yourself in the future. (b) Write a report of 250 to 300 words about two careers that interest you and how specific classes and good scholarship in general will help you achieve your career goals.

- **Common Myth:** Education ends on the day you graduate from high school, trade school, or college.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Your formal schooling builds the foundation for lifelong self-education across both career fields and personal interests (p. 61).

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- You are now heading down the home stretch toward your Scholarship merit badge (p. 61).

- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Replacing tool handles involves boring out old wood, shaping the new hickory or ash tenon to fit the tool eye, and driving wedges. Proper grain orientation along the line of swing prevents premature handle fracture under heavy impact.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Head Securing: Secure the damaged steel chisel vertically inside heavy serrated bench vise jaws.

- **Common Myth:** Any hardwood branch from the farm woodlot works reliably as a sledgehammer handle.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Steep grain runout on replacement wood handles creates weak points that snap under heavy shock.

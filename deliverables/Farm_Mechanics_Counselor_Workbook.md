### Req 1: Farm Shop Safety Systems
**Official Requirement 1 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Discuss with your counselor the safety equipment, tools, and clothing used while checking or repairing farm equipment. Use this equipment, tools, and/or clothing (when needed or called for) in meeting the requirements for this merit badge. (b) Draw a plan showing a well-equipped farm shop. Point out the shop's mandatory safety devices and features. (c) Find all the universal warning and safety symbols on a piece of equipment and explain what they mean. (d) Describe what a safety data sheet (SDS) is and tell why it is used. Obtain the SDS for any engine coolant, oil, grease, fuel, hydraulic or transmission fluid, or other flammable or hazardous materials you use in meeting the requirements for this merit badge.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Farm Mechanics Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 38, p. 56)* • **Group Organizer:** Field Sketch & Diagram Canvas
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Remove all rings, wristwatches, and loose clothing before servicing rotating power takeoff shafts or belt drives.
- **Common Myth:** Standard sunglasses provide sufficient optical protection when grinding or handling caustic shop chemicals.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Personal protective equipment must be chosen based on specific chemical and mechanical shop hazards.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Eye Protection Standards: Impact resistant safety glasses shield eyes against high speed flying metal fragments.
- Protective Footwear Guidelines: Steel toe leather boots guard feet against heavy dropped machine parts.
- Respiratory Mask Requirements: Dual cartridge respirators filter toxic solvent vapors during parts degreasing procedures.
- Hand Safety Rules: Heavy leather gloves protect hands during metal grinding and torch cutting.

### Req 2: Agricultural Machinery Power Systems
**Official Requirement 2 (Parent Overview):** Explain how power is produced or transferred in a: (a) Diesel engine (b) Hydraulic system (c) Transmission or any other power system
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Farm Mechanics Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 50, p. 56)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Relieve residual hydraulic line pressure before cracking open any fittings or disconnecting implement quick couplers.
- **Common Myth:** Diesel engines ignite fuel using high-voltage spark plugs similar to gasoline engines.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** High compression heats trapped air above the ignition point of injected fuel oil.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Compression Ignition Principles: Diesel cylinders compress air until extreme heat ignites atomized fuel oil.
- Incompressible hydraulic oil transmits force through high pressure lines to cylinders.
- Planetary gearsets multiply engine torque to rotate drive axles under load.
- Fluid Reservoir Purpose: Reservoirs store excess oil while dissipating heat and releasing entrained air.

### Req 3: Farm Tool Maintenance Techniques
**Official Requirement 3 (Parent Overview):** Do TWO of the following: (a) Replace the handle of any tool found on the farm. (b) Organize a tool rack or a storage system for nails, bolts, nuts, and washers. (c) Using a hand file, properly dress the mushroomed head of a chisel or punch. (d) Using a hand file, correctly dress a screwdriver tip.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Farm Mechanics Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 24, p. 25)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Clamp struck tools in a heavy bench vise before using a file on mushroomed metal heads.
- **Common Myth:** A mushroomed chisel head provides a larger, safer striking target for shop hammers.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** A mushroomed chisel head can shatter under hammer blows and send metal shrapnel flying.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Wood Wedge Fitting: Hardwood wedges spread tool handle tenons firmly against steel eye walls.
- Fastener Sizing Systems: Thread pitch gauges identify bolt thread counts before hardware cabinet sorting.
- Chisel Dressing Technique: Hand files dress mushroomed tool heads back to original chamfered edge profiles.
- Flat file strokes create parallel screwdriver tip faces that grip screw slots.

### Req 4: Machinery Preventive Maintenance Operations
**Official Requirement 4 (Parent Overview):** Do ONE of the following: (a) On an engine-powered machine: Grease all fittings, change the oil and oil filter, clean the air filter, clean the radiator fins, and replace the fuel filters. (b) For any engine-powered machine, create a preoperational checklist; include checking the engine coolant, engine oil, hydraulic and/or transmission fluid, and battery voltage (using a voltmeter). Using your checklist, conduct a preoperational check of that machinery or equipment. (c) Prepare any farm machine for winter storage.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Farm Mechanics Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 56, p. 58)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Lock out engine ignitions and lower all hydraulic implements to ground contact before greasing equipment.
- **Common Myth:** Pumping grease into a fitting until grease overflows guarantees the joint is properly lubricated.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Pumping grease too rapidly or overfilling joints ruptures rubber dust seals and admits grit.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Manual grease guns deliver specific ounce volumes per lever stroke during lubrication.
- Heat and soot deplete engine oil additive packages during heavy fieldwork.
- Compressed air bursts can tear microscopic filter paper pores, allowing dirt entry.
- Cooling Fin Clearing: Clogged radiator fins reduce airflow, causing severe engine overheating during tillage.

- Tapered pipe threads require liquid paste sealant rather than shredded teflon tape.

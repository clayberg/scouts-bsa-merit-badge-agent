### Req 1: Gardening Hazard Management Protocol
**Official Requirement 1 (Parent Overview):** Do the following: (a) Explain to your counselor the most likely hazards associated with gardening and what you should do to anticipate, help prevent, mitigate, and respond to these hazards. (b) Discuss the prevention of and treatment for health concerns that could occur while gardening, including cuts, scratches, puncture wounds, insect bites, anaphylactic shock, heat reactions, and reactions from exposure to pesticides and fertilizers.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Gardening Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 42, p. 70)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Keep rake tines turned down toward the soil surface and store all cutting blades sheathed or hung securely.
- **Common Myth:** Gardening tools cause minimal bodily injury because manual soil cultivation requires little mechanical force.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Proper ergonomic posture and sharp hand bypass pruners reduce physical strain while preventing bacterial cross contamination in cuts.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Tool Storage Safety: Place garden rakes and hoes with tines pointed downward into soil.
- Hydration Maintenance: Drink eight ounces of cool potable water every thirty minutes outdoors.
- Tetanus Protection Protocol: Clean puncture wounds immediately with soap and water before seeking medical care.
- Personal Protective Equipment: Wear heavy leather gloves and broad brimmed hats under direct sun.

### Req 2: Vegetable and Flower Production
**Official Requirement 2 (Parent Overview):** Do the following, and discuss your observations throughout the process with your counselor: (a) Grow six vegetables, three from seeds and three from seedlings, through harvest. (b) Grow six flowers, three from seeds and three from seedlings, through flowering.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Gardening Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 45, p. 47)* • **Group Organizer:** Concept & Examples Notes
- **Common Myth:** Indoor vegetable seedlings can move straight into full outdoor sun without intermediate acclimation.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Seedlings need gradual outdoor hardening over one week to withstand ambient solar intensity.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Bury vegetable seeds at depth equal to three times their width.
- Seedling Hardening Process: Acclimate indoor starts outdoors in partial shade over seven consecutive days.
- Flower Bed Mulching: Spread two inches of straw mulch to conserve moisture and suppress weeds.
- Harvest Timing Check: Pick tender pod vegetables early in morning to preserve crisp sweetness.

### Req 3: Plant Crop Nutritional Values
**Official Requirement 3 (Parent Overview):** Give the nutritional value of the following: (a) Three root or tuber crops (b) Three vegetables that bear above the ground (c) Three fruits

- **Pamphlet Fact:** Carrot roots synthesize beta carotene while storing carbohydrates for biennial plant survival.

**Official Pamphlet Summary:**
- Carrot Nutritional Value: Taproots deliver abundant beta carotene precursors for human vitamin A synthesis.
- Broccoli Floret Chemistry: Aboveground green clusters supply significant dietary fiber along with ascorbic acid.
- Berry Nutrient Content: Ripe strawberries provide dense antioxidant compounds alongside high bioavailable vitamin C.
- Potato Carbohydrate Density: Underground tubers store complex starches accompanied by potassium in outer skins.

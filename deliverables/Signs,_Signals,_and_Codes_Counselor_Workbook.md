- **Common Myth:** Braille characters are embossed outlines shaped like standard capital Roman letters so readers can trace the letter shapes.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Every standard Braille cell is arranged as a vertical rectangle two dots wide by three dots high, numbered 1-2-3 down the left column and 4-5-6 down the right column, yielding 63 possible dot combinations plus a blank space (p. 6).

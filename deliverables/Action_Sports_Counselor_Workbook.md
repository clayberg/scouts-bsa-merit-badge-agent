## 2. Pre-Class Prerequisite Checklist ('Bring Proof to Class')

- [ ] **Req 5 (Demonstrate):** Do the following: (a) Demonstrate three intermediate maneuvers or tricks approved by your counselor in a supervised skatepark or snow terrain park. (b) Explain Leave No Trace and outdoor stewardship principles at mountain resorts and community skateparks, and explore careers or volunteer roles (such as National Ski Patrol, adaptive sports instructor, or equipment engineer) in action sports.

## 3. Hands-On Skill Station Verification Rubric

- [ ] **Station Req 2:** Do the following: (a) Explain the Skier/Snowboarder Responsibility Code or Skatepark Etiquette and Safety Rules, and why each rule is vital to rider and bystander safety. (b) Demonstrate a warm-up, stretching, and conditioning routine for action sports.
- [ ] **Station Req 3:** Identify the parts of your skateboard (deck, grip tape, trucks, kingpin, bushings, wheels, bearings) or snowboard (nose, tail, edges, base, camber/rocker, bindings, leash) and demonstrate routine inspection, cleaning, and maintenance.
- [ ] **Station Req 4:** Choose ONE option (Skateboarding OR Snowboarding) and demonstrate fundamental riding skills: (a) Stance (regular vs. goofy), mounting/dismounting, pushing/gliding on flat terrain, and controlled stopping (foot braking, heel/toe edge stop, or tail scrape). (b) Carving turns (frontside and backside), weight transfer, falling safely, and navigating slopes or skatepark transitions under full control.

**Source:** *Official Scouting America Action Sports Merit Badge Pamphlet* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Skill Demonstration Checklist):** Req 3 Hands-On Skill Station & EDGE Verification Checklist
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** Discard cracked skateboard decks and sheared kingpin bolts immediately, as sudden component separation during riding causes catastrophic wipeouts.
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** How does changing the durometer of your skateboard bushings or snowboard base flex profile alter your turning radius?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Adjusting the central kingpin nut alters truck turning resistance and high-speed directional stability.
  - Edge Tuning: Diamond stones remove microscopic burrs from steel edges to maintain sharp carving bite.
  - Bearing Service: Solvents strip contaminated grease from ball bearings before fresh synthetic lubricant is introduced.
  - Hot waxing hydrates porous polyethylene snowboard bases, preventing friction burn and dry oxidation.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Hardware Inspection: Check mounting bolts and axle nuts for stripped threads or structural micro-cracks.
  - Step 2 - Component Cleaning: Wipe bearings with citrus solvent and dry them completely before applying lubrication.
  - Step 3 - Edge Dressing: Run a deburring stone smoothly down metal side edges to remove oxidized burrs.
  - Step 4 - Torque Verification: Tighten binding baseplate screws firmly in a crisscross pattern using a hand tool.
- **Common Myth:** Skateboarders believe applying petroleum-based spray lubricants like household water dispersant protects skateboard wheel bearings.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Household water dispersants dissolve internal bearing grease, attract abrasive road grit, and cause rapid bearing seizure at high speeds.

### Req 3: Camera Mechanics and Exposure Operation
**Official Requirement 3:** Explain the basic parts and operation of a camera. Explain how an exposure is made when you take a picture.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Photography Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 39, p. 43)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 3 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** What sequence occurs inside the camera body when the shutter release button trips?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Optical Focus: Glass lens elements bend light rays to converge sharply onto digital sensor planes.
  - Shutter curtains open briefly to regulate the total duration of light sensor exposure.
  - Silicon sensor photodiodes convert focused light photons into measurable electrical charge values.
  - Aperture Diaphragm: Overlapping metal blades expand or contract to control incoming optical light volume.
- **Step-by-Step Facilitation & Station Procedure:**
  - Step 1 - Body Inspection: Check the battery charge and insert an empty digital memory card.
  - Step 2 - Lens Mounting: Align lens mounting dots and lock the barrel firmly onto camera mounts.
  - Step 3 - Focus Acquisition: Press the shutter button halfway to lock automatic focus onto target subjects.
  - Step 4 - Exposure Release: Depress the shutter button fully to expose the image sensor to light.
- **Common Myth:** Camera lenses contain the digital sensor chips that convert light into electronic files.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** Depressing the shutter release button opens mechanical curtains, exposing electronic sensors to incoming light.

**[Parent Directive]:** *Do TWO of the following, then share your work with your counselor*
**Official Requirement 4b:** Photograph one subject from two different light sources, artificial and natural.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America Photography Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 31, p. 37)* • **Scout Workbook Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 4b Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix
- **Socratic Prompt (Ask Scouts First):** For photography Requirement 4b (Photograph one subject from two different light sources, artificial and natural), what key BSA pamphlet standard and safety or verification step must you explain or demonstrate to your counselor?
- **Official Pamphlet Summary & Expected Answer Key:**
  - Contrasting natural ambient light with artificial flash highlights variations in color temperature and shadows. Photographers must analyze shadow hardness and color casts produced by different light sources.

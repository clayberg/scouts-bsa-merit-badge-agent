Find out how the area has changed over the past 50 years and try to explain why. (d) Take an active part in a program about an event or person in American history. ### Req 1: Paraphrasing the Declaration of Independence
**Official Requirement 1:** Read the Declaration of Independence. Pay close attention to the section that begins with "We hold these truths to be self-evident" and ends with "to provide new Guards for their future security." Rewrite that section in your own words, making it as easy to understand as possible. Then, share your writing with your counselor and discuss the importance of the Declaration to all Americans.
**Source:** *Official Scouting America American Heritage Merit Badge Pamphlet (p. 14, p. 15)* • **Organizer (Concept & Examples Notes):** Req 1 Structured Technical Study & Explanation Matrix (Declaration of Independence Pay Close)
> **Guide to Safe Scouting Warning:** BSA Youth Protection & Buddy System (Req 1): Complete all Paraphrasing the Declaration of Independence discussions and demonstrations with Two-Deep Leadership and your patrol buddy.
- **Common Myth:** The Declaration of Independence was the legal document that established the three branches of the federal government.
- **Pamphlet Fact:** The Declaration of Independence explained the colonists' basic human rights and the causes for separating from Great Britain, whereas the Constitution later established the government framework (p. 14).

# Scope: Scouts BSA Merit Badge Counselor Workbench

## 1. Problem statement
Volunteer Scouts BSA Merit Badge Counselors typically spend 8 to 15 hours building slide decks, Scout workbooks, lesson plans, and parent prerequisite letters for each of the 149 official Merit Badges (`143` active official Merit Badges, `5` Scouting America Test Lab Pilot badges: *Auctioneering*, *Dance*, *Physics*, *Psychology*, *Wildland Fire Management*, plus *Sewing and Needlework*, and `1` discontinued historical badge: *Citizenship in Society*, annotated with a red `🚫 Discontinued` indicator in the Patch Gallery). Standard single-prompt LLM slide generators do a poor job on Merit Badges: they summarize requirements down to four generic bullets, cut off multi-part sub-requirements (`1a`, `1b`, `2b1`, `3a-3q`), mix up requirement years, and repeat the same stock diagram across slides.

This project builds a multi-agent curriculum workbench on the Google Agent Development Kit (ADK) for Python (`scouts-bsa-merit-badge-agent`). It generates widescreen (`16:9`) PowerPoint decks (`.pptx`), 4-pillar sub-requirement-unrolled fillable Scout workbooks (`.pdf` AcroForm, interactive `contenteditable` `.html`, and `.md` per `GTA §7.0.3.2`), multi-page 1-Page Class Preparation Pages (`CPP` with `0%` dropped requirement text), session agendas, and 3-Stage Youth Protection (YPT) parent communication packs grounded in the official BSA Merit Badge Pamphlet:

1. **5-tier pamphlet and regional research (`src/agents/researcher.py`)**: Extracts the full requirement and sub-requirement tree verbatim from `scouting.org` and official BSA Pamphlet PDFs (`PyMuPDF`), locks the requirement wording with a SHA-256 hash (`compute_canonical_pamphlet_hash()`), classifies each requirement by how it is taught (`IN_CLASS_DISCUSSION`, `HANDS_ON_SKILL_STATION`, `PREREQUISITE_CAMPOUT_HOME`), and resolves the counselor's City, State, or 5-digit ZIP code (`resolve_counselor_location()`) across all 51 U.S. jurisdictions (`_STATE_CIVIC_AND_FIELD_REGISTRY`) to regional NOAA National Weather Service offices, local terrain hazards, and state agencies.
2. **12-archetype slide planner, Archetype-Differentiated 5-Part Non-Redundant Facilitator Guide, Hybrid PowerPoint Template Engine, Global Deck Layout Style Engine (`DeckStyleConfig`), and unified visual pipeline (`src/agents/planner.py`, `src/agents/beautifier.py`, `src/tools/pptx_builder.py`, `src/tools/template_engine.py`)**: Maps each requirement onto 12 structured `python-pptx` layouts with the **5-Part Non-Redundant Facilitator Guide** (`[PACING & MODALITY]`, `[ASK SCOUTS FIRST]`, `[EXPECTED PAMPHLET ANSWER KEY]`, `[COMMON MISCONCEPTION TO CORRECT]`, `[DEMONSTRATION & SIGN-OFF RUBRIC]`), scoping child slides (`Sub-Requirement Deep Dive Part k`, `SPLIT_VISUAL_EXPLAINER`, `STEP_BY_STEP_PROCEDURE_4CARD`, `SOCRATIC_CHECKPOINT_QUIZ`) to their exact sub-requirements (`1a-1d` vs. `1e-1g`, `3a-3q`) and rotating `pamphlet_excerpts` so consecutive slides never repeat boilerplate notes. ## 4. Why Custom Pro-Code Google ADK vs. Out-of-the-Box Gemini Enterprise (Agentspace) or NotebookLM? (Build-vs-Buy Defense)

Enterprise architecture panels rightly ask why Scouting America should deploy a custom pro-code **7-Agent Google ADK Workbench** rather than uploading an 80-page Merit Badge Pamphlet PDF into out-of-the-box **NotebookLM** or a no-code **Gemini Enterprise (Agentspace)** assistant. While NotebookLM and Agentspace excel at conversational Q&A over documents, they cannot satisfy six hard domain, geometric, cryptographic, and field-operations invariants required by Scouts BSA advancement standards:
